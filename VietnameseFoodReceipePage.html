Vietnamese Pho

Brief Description

This is page is gonna teach you how to cook Vietnamese Pho like a local in Vietnam. Follow this guide and it will show the ingredients you need, the tools needed for this operation, time spent on each instruction, and steps on cooking the pho.

Ingredients

  • Beef Broth: 4 cups of high-quality, low-sodium store-bought beef broth
  • 1 small yellow onion (sliced)
  • Pho Spices: 2 whole star anise and 1 small cinnamon stick
  • Fast Seasoning: 1-2 tablespoons of fish sauce and 1 teaspoon of sugar
  • Rice Noodles: 8 ounces of dried flat noodles
  • Quick Beef: 1/2 pound of flank steak, sirloin, or ribeye, sliced as thin as possible across the grain
  • Greenery: 1/2 cup of fresh cilantro and 2 sliced green onions.
  • The Finish: 1 cup of fresh bean sprouts, 1 fresh lime (cut into wedges), and Sriracha or Hoisin sauce for dipping.

Instructions

  1. Infuse:
    2. Simmer in the broth, onion, star anise, and cinnamon for 15 minutes.
  2. Boil Noodles: Cook rice noodles according to the package, drain, and put them in bowls.
  3. Season: Strain the hot broth, then stir in the fish sauce and sugar.
  4. Assemble: Layer the paper-thin raw beef and fresh herbs right on top of the noodles.
  5. Pour: Ladle the boiling broth over the meat to cook it instantly. Serve with lime and sauces.
